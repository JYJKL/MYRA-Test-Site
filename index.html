Leadership

Margarethe Baillou

M.Y.R.A.’s founder, Margarethe Baillou, has been fortunate to live across Europe, in both Americas and Asia which has allowed her to study a wide range of cultures and our awe-inspiring natural world. She delights in film’s ability to win the race against time, while being highly aware of the powerful impact the audio-visual medium has on society.

The team behind M.Y.R.A. is multicultural, polyglot and diverse. They operate in multiple languages and have lived in numerous countries across the globe. M.Y.R.A. has gen-z, gen-alpha and canine in-house focus groups who inspire quality, meaningful work.

Together, the team gravitates toward responsible storytelling that is global in an effort to unite, rich in language, humanistic and inclusive, never portraying graphic violence in service of audiences’ mental health.

"Film has a profound impact on society—we choose to use that power to unite."
