Leadership

Margarethe Baillou

M.Y.R.A.'s founder, Margarethe Baillou, has lived across Europe, the Americas, and Asia, studying diverse cultures and the natural world.

With a firm belief in cinema’s capacity to win the race against time, she leads a polyglot, multicultural team dedicated to crafting humanistic, inclusive cinema free from graphic violence.

"Film has a profound impact on society—we choose to use that power to unite."
